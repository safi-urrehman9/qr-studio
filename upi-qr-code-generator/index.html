      Questions

      Which apps can pay with it?

      Any UPI app, including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm and BHIM.

      Can the customer enter the amount?

      Yes. Remove the Amount field (✕) and the payer types the amount in their app.

      Is this the same as my bank’s merchant QR?

      It uses the standard UPI payment link format, which suits personal and small-business use. Registered merchants may get extra features (such as settlement reports) from their bank’s own QR.

      How do I stay safe with payment QR codes?

      Always test your code by scanning it yourself, and check printed codes regularly: scammers sometimes stick their own code over a shop’s.

      Is it free? Do the codes expire?

      Free, with no sign-up and no watermark. The codes are standard static QR codes: the content lives inside the code itself, not behind a redirect link we control, so they keep working forever, even if this website disappears.
