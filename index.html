Questions

Are my images or data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser: the image is read, edited and turned into a new QR code on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server. The first time you use AI field naming, your browser downloads a small 23 MB model once and keeps it cached.

Is it free? Do the codes expire?

Free, with no sign-up and no watermark. The codes are standard static QR codes: the content lives inside the code itself, not behind a redirect link we control, so they keep working forever, even if this website disappears.

How is this different from other QR code sites?

Most QR sites only make codes, and many upload your files or put your codes behind their own redirect links. QR Studio opens existing codes, explains what they do, warns about scams, and lets you edit and re-create them, all on your device.
