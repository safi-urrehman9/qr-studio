      Questions

      How many images can I read at once?

      There is no fixed limit. Hundreds work fine on a normal laptop; it depends on your device’s memory.

      What’s in the spreadsheet?

      One row per image: file name, whether a code was found, its type (link, Wi-Fi, contact…) and the full contents.

      Which image types work?

      Anything your browser can open: PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF and screenshots. Safari can also open iPhone HEIC photos.

      Are my images or data uploaded anywhere?

      No. Everything runs in your browser: the image is read, edited and turned into a new QR code on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server. The first time you use AI field naming, your browser downloads a small 23 MB model once and keeps it cached.
